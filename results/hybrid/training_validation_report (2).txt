Interpretation
--------------
Training loss decreased across all three epochs, from 1.259200 to
1.108900. Validation loss also decreased, from 1.216067 to 1.151128.
This indicates improving prediction of the target SOAP text on the
training and validation data during this run.

These losses are token-level training metrics. They do not, on their
own, establish SOAP-note factual accuracy, clinical quality, or safety.

The HYBRID candidate-fact extractor uses fixed rules and a medication
lookup table; it is not trained as part of this run. The LoRA adapter
fine-tunes the language model to generate SOAP notes from the transcript
and extracted candidate facts.
